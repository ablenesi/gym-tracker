How to use

  • Open Sessions. Today's or your next session is highlighted in orange; tap any session header to expand it.
  • For each exercise enter Weight (kg), Reps (on your last, hardest set) and Sets (pre-filled with the target). Notes are optional.
  • Plank: Reps = seconds held, leave Weight blank. Walking lunges: Reps = per leg.
  • Everything saves automatically on this device/browser. Use Export regularly as a backup, and Import to move to another phone or browser.
  • The grey hint shows what you did last time. ↑ add weight means last time you hit the top of the rep range.

Progression rule (double progression)

  • Work inside the target rep range (e.g. 3 × 8–12).
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets with good form, add the smallest weight increment next session (one plate on the stack, +1–2 kg dumbbells, +5–10 kg leg press).
  • Weeks 1–2: leave 2–3 reps in reserve. From week 3, push a little closer (1–2 in reserve).
  • Take a lighter week every 6–8 weeks: about half the sets, lighter weights.

Safety note

  • Eat some protein 1–2 h before training and drink water during the session.
  • If you feel dizzy, faint or unusually drained, stop and check with your GP or prescriber before the next session.
  • General fitness information, not medical advice.
