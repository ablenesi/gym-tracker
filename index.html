How to use

  • Open Sessions. Today's or your next session is highlighted in orange; tap any session header to expand it.
  • For each exercise enter Weight (kg), Reps (on your last, hardest set) and Sets (pre-filled with the target).
  • Plank: Reps = seconds held, leave Weight blank. Walking lunges: Reps = per leg.
  • Everything saves automatically on this device/browser. Use Export regularly as a backup, and Import to move to another phone or browser.
  • The grey hint shows what you did last time. The orange chip under Weight (Seconds for plank) is the suggested next weight: +2.5 kg on machines/cables or +2 kg per dumbbell when you hit the top of the range, the same weight when you're in range (or 1–2 reps short), and one step lighter if you fell further short. Tap it to fill the field, or type your own.
  • Log reps per set in the small boxes (one per set; change Sets to add or remove boxes). The chip next to them suggests reps for each set: the top of the range for every set after you hit it on all sets (at the new weight), the same reps as last time when in range, one fewer per set if you were 3+ short, or the middle of the range the first time. The lowest set counts as the session's reps for progress and hints.
  • Weekly pattern: Mon/Wed/Fri lifting (A/B/C), Tue Peloton HIIT (P), Thu yoga (Y), Sat core (K), Sunday rest. Those cards show the session plan and simple log fields (duration, output, effort…); they save and sync like everything else and get their own summary under Progress.

Progression rule (double progression)

  • Work inside the target rep range (e.g. 3 × 8–12).
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets with good form, add the smallest weight increment next session (one plate on the stack, +1–2 kg dumbbells, +5–10 kg leg press).
  • Weeks 1–2: leave 2–3 reps in reserve. From week 3, push a little closer (1–2 in reserve).
  • Take a lighter week every 6–8 weeks: about half the sets, lighter weights.

Safety note

  • Eat some protein 1–2 h before training and drink water during the session.
  • If you feel dizzy, faint or unusually drained, stop and check with your GP or prescriber before the next session.
  • General fitness information, not medical advice.

Backup & data

CSV uses these columns: Date, Day, Exercise, Target, Weight, Reps (lowest set), Reps by set (e.g. 12/11/10), Sets, then Duration, Output (kJ), Avg HR, Effort and Rounds for Peloton (Day P), yoga (Y) and core (K) rows – the spreadsheet's Log sheet columns plus Reps by set.
